The Formula

Vegas line as baseline. Sport-specific adjustments layered on top.

Expected Margin

Vegas Implied Margin + Injury Residual (beyond Vegas pricing) + Rest Advantage + Composite Team Quality (Elo 50% + Power 25% + Momentum 15% + H2H 10%) + Referee Home Bias (zeroed on neutral sites) + Travel Fatigue + Off/Def Mismatch + Situational Spot Score + Public Betting Fade + Sharp Money Signal + Starting Pitcher/Goalie Quality

Expected Total

Vegas Total Line − Weather Penalty (outdoor only — retractable roof venues classified) + Referee Over/Under Tendency (reduced for tournament crews) + Pace Divergence (discounted 60% for tournament games) + Total Line Movement + Big Spread Under Push (blowouts suppress totals — stronger in college)
What changed: We replaced four separate correlated metrics (Elo, Power, Momentum, H2H) with one composite team quality score — eliminating double-counting. All adjustments are now weighted for residual edge beyond what Vegas already priced. Neutral site detection zeroes out home-court factors for tournament games. Eight sport-specific calibrations, continuously recalibrated.
